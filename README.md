# BicknellWindTool

**BicknellWindTool** is a spatial decision-support tool designed to support biodiversity-informed planning of wind energy development in Bicknell’s Thrush (*Catharus bicknelli*) breeding habitat.

The tool builds upon the methodology and findings presented in:

**Wind energy development alters breeding habitat of the Bicknell’s Thrush, a threatened range-restricted boreal songbird: evidence for opportunities in biodiversity-informed spatial planning**  
Nassivera *et al.* (2026) — under review

## Overview

BicknellWindTool integrates spatial information on wind energy potential and Bicknell’s Thrush breeding habitat to evaluate the potential ecological impacts of wind energy development and explore alternative development areas.

The application is intended as an **early-stage planning and decision-support tool**, allowing potential conflicts between wind energy development and high-priority breeding habitat to be identified before more detailed engineering, economic, regulatory, and site-specific constraints are incorporated into the planning process.

## Main functionalities

BicknellWindTool allows users to:

- import and define a wind energy project area;
- quantify the overlap between proposed wind energy development and Bicknell’s Thrush breeding habitat;
- distinguish impacts according to habitat conservation priority;
- evaluate changes in landscape metrics associated with wind energy development;
- explore alternative development areas based on different planning perspectives;
- compare the ecological impacts of the proposed development area with those of alternative development areas;
- visualize results through interactive spatial outputs;
- in future versions, generate standardized outputs to support interpretation and decision-making.

## Planning scenarios

Alternative development areas can be explored using different weighting schemes representing contrasting planning perspectives:

- **Reference scenario:** equal overall importance given to wind energy potential and ecological constraints;
- **Development scenario:** greater importance given to wind energy potential;
- **Conservation scenario:** greater importance given to ecological constraints.

These scenarios are intended to explore potential trade-offs rather than reproduce the complete wind farm siting process.

## Scientific basis

The ecological component of BicknellWindTool is based on spatial predictions of Bicknell’s Thrush breeding habitat and associated habitat-priority classes.

The spatial planning framework combines three dimensions:

1. **Wind energy potential**
2. **Breeding habitat priority**
3. **Local habitat aggregation**

Together, these dimensions allow locations to be ranked according to their relative suitability for wind energy development while explicitly accounting for potential impacts on breeding habitat.
